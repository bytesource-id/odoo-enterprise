  • In Odoo 15.0 web assets from custom modules must be declared using the new model 'ir.asset', done in the module's manifest. The following modules include asset views that should be declared according to the new standard. Please make sure your assets are moved out of the views and your modules' manifests have an "assets" key to define the desired bundles of each asset. Discuss

  • PLEASE NOTE:

    Mass mailing channels are no longer displayed in the Discuss app by default starting from Odoo 15.0.
    They become mail groups and are moved to http://localhost:8069/groups, where you can subscribe and follow discussions.
    If you prefer to keep a channel in the Discuss app after the upgrade, go to the channel settings and uncheck the "send messages by email" option.
